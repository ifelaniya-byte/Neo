## Selection Strategy

Select systems based on current needs:
- **High parameter count:** Compression systems (1-20)
- **Slow inference:** Inference optimization (21-40)
- **Poor generalization:** Advanced learning (41-60)
- **Hardware constraints:** Hardware optimization (61-80)
- **Deployment needs:** Deployment optimization (81-100)
